# News Grasp DeepDive Podcast Cover - Image2.0 Prompt

## 方針

DeepDive Podcast では、日付・記事タイトル・カテゴリごとに画像を作り分けない。全エピソードで共通利用できる、番組ブランド用の静的カバー画像を 1 枚だけ作成する。

今回は「後輩女性社員が質問し、先輩男性社員がフランクに答える」解説対談コンテンツなので、Podcast画像も二人が会話している印象を主役にする。前回の朗読風イメージからは、黒背景、知的なAI/ニュース感、落ち着いたビジネス調、少し抽象的な線画表現を継承する。

## Prompt

Create a reusable square podcast cover illustration for a Japanese business news analysis program called "News Grasp DeepDive". This is a permanent brand cover for all episodes, not a daily episode thumbnail.

Canvas: 1:1 square, 3000 x 3000 px. Style: premium Japanese business editorial illustration, calm consulting report aesthetic, near-black ink background, warm off-white highlights, deep green label accents, muted gold details, subtle AI network line motifs inherited from a narration-style cover, crisp but friendly composition.

Main scene: two Japanese office colleagues having a relaxed explanatory conversation at a small table. On the left, a young calm female employee in business casual attire is asking a thoughtful question, holding a notebook or tablet. On the right, a mature friendly male senior employee is answering with an approachable expression, slightly leaning forward. Their posture should feel professional, warm, and conversational, not like a formal interview.

Podcast context: add a subtle tabletop microphone or small recorder only as a secondary detail, not as the main subject. The focus must be the two-person dialogue and the sense of learning through conversation.

Visual continuity from the narration cover: use a dark background with a faint blue-cyan AI network pattern, soft white linework, and quiet glowing nodes, but make it less technical and more human. Blend the abstract AI motif into the background behind the two people. Use restrained gold rules and deep green editorial accents so it still feels like News Grasp / DeepDive.

Text requirement: include only stable brand text. Keep it clean and readable:
- "News Grasp"
- "DeepDive"
- optionally "Dialogue Podcast"

Do not include any date, episode title, category label, read time, issue number, company name, article headline, or variable placeholder text. The image must remain valid for every DeepDive Podcast episode.

Composition: square cover, strong central focus on the two characters, brand title in the upper or lower area with generous safe margin, no crowded text. The image should remain readable as a small podcast thumbnail. Use a sophisticated Japanese editorial mood rather than a cute cartoon mood.

Negative prompt: anime idol, manga exaggeration, cute mascot, childish cartoon, photorealistic people, stock photo office, formal press interview, TV studio, large podcast microphone dominating the image, headphones as the main motif, neon cyberpunk, purple gradient, blue tech glow overload, cluttered dashboard, real company logos, fake company logos, episode title, date, numbers, random alphabet strings, watermark, unreadable text.

## 生成後の確認観点

- 1:1 の正方形カバーになっている
- 後輩女性社員と先輩男性社員の二人が、自然に会話している構図になっている
- Podcastらしさはあるが、マイクや機材が主役になっていない
- 日付・記事タイトル・企業名など、日次要素が入っていない
- 「News Grasp」「DeepDive」以外の文字が増えすぎていない
- 前回の朗読風イメージの黒背景・AIネットワーク感を継承しつつ、人間同士の解説対談として見える
- スマホの小さいサムネイルでも番組名と二人の会話構図が分かる
